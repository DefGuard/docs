# Desktop Client

In order to provide more detailed information, please do the following:

#### Enable DEBUG logs in the client

1. If you don't have **Defguard** in full view mode, open **Defguard** from tray view, and click **Open Defguard**
<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/tray-view-open-defguard-marked.png" alt="" width="50%"></figure>

2. Go to settings tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/settings-tab-full-view.png" alt=""></figure>

3. Click on currently selected opton in **Logging threshold** section.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/logging-threshold-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

4. Choose `Debug` from list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/debug-threshold-marked.png" alt=""></figure>


#### Gather more logs before sending

Before submitting the logs, after you have changed the logging to debug, please repeat any actions that have caused any problems.

#### Collecting the client logs

1. If you don't have **Defguard** in full view mode, open **Defguard** from tray view, and click **Open Defguard**
<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/tray-view-open-defguard-marked.png" alt="" width="50%"></figure>

2. Go to Log tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/log-tab-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

3. Click on the filter on the left.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/log-range-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

4. Set filter to `Debug`.
 
<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/debug-option-selected.png" alt=""></figure>

5. Click on the filter on the right.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/all-option-menu-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

6. Set filter to `All`.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/all-option-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

7. Click on actions menu.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/actions-menu-marked.png" alt=""></figure>

8. Download logs

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/download-actions-menu.png" alt=""></figure>

9. Save file.
