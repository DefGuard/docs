# Managing your Instance

In this guide you will learn how to manage your instances. If you haven't added your instance yet, follow [this guide](instance-adding.md#adding-instance-in-defguard)

## Changing traffic routing method after first connection

1. Open Defguard.
2. Go to **Instances** in the app menu.
3. Press & Hold the **Connect** button next to the location you want to update.
4. When the popup appears, look for the option **Select Traffic Routing**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/defguard-mobile-connect-traffic.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. You will see this:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/defguard-mobile-traffic-panel.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Choose method and click **Connect**

## Changing MFA method after first connection

1. Go to **Instances** in the app menu.
2. Press & Hold **Connect** button next to location name and wait until popup shows.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/defguard-mobile-connect-mfa (1).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Select MFA Method**.
4. You will see this:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/defguard-mobile-mfa-panel.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Choose method and click **Connect**
